Zenvert Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Zenvert Android app (“Zenvert”, “the app”) handles information. Zenvert is provided by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY NAME] (“we”, “us”).

1. Summary

Zenvert does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. The app works fully offline and has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal data. Zenvert does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or any other personal information.

3. Information stored on your device

To work properly, the app saves a small amount of data locally on your device only:

  • Your theme choice (light or dark mode).
  • Conversions that you choose to save with the ★ button (for example, “1 km = 0.621371 mi”).

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. You can delete saved conversions inside the app (Saved conversions → Clear all), or remove all app data by clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app.

4. Permissions

Zenvert does not request any Android permissions. It does not access the internet.

5. Third-party services

Zenvert does not include advertising networks, analytics tools, crash-reporting services or other third-party SDKs that collect user data.

6. Children’s privacy

Because we collect no personal information from anyone, Zenvert does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country).

7. Accuracy of conversions

Zenvert is intended for general everyday use. We aim for accurate results, but you should verify values before relying on them for medical, engineering, legal, financial or other critical purposes.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this Privacy Policy, we will post the updated version on this page and change the “Last updated” date. If the app’s data practices ever change, this policy will be updated before the change takes effect.
